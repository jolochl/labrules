Visitors planning to use the IC S\,2 facility for microscopy of live S\,2 samples must provide all the information necessary for the registration of their project with the authorities. Failure to do so will lead to delay and possible cancellation of their experiment. Visitors are not permitted to work in the S\,2 facility and carry out microscopy of S\,2 specimens while not supervised by S\,2-trained IC staff.
