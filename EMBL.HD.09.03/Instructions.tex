All users must attend a safety briefing before being admitted to the S\,2 areas. Staff will be briefed by the Health \& Safety Office during one of the EMBL’s monthly S\,2 safety briefings. The contents and times of the briefing must be recorded and signed by the instructed person. The briefing must be refreshed every 12 months. Safety relevant changes to the genetic engineering facility will revoke existing and require new briefing. For the temporary S\,2 areas (microscope rooms), records of the microscopy experiment must be kept, including the identity of the S 2 specimen, the user's name, the microscope used, the room number, the start and end dates, and the reference number of the S2 project the specimen belongs to. All records must be forwarded to the Health \& Safety Office.\\
\newline
Project records must be kept, including the name of the donor organism, the recipient organism, and the transferred gene. These records must be forwarded as a completed 'form Z' document to the HSO upon request.
